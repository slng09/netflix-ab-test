Recommendation and caveats

Recommend
    Limitations
    • All data is simulated with assumed effect sizes, so the numbers show the method, not a real Netflix outcome.
    • 28 days is too short to measure retention. A long-term holdout of about 5% of members would settle it.
    • Members share household profiles, so randomising by account may leak effects between viewers.
    • Device and region cuts are exploratory: nine segments means more chances of a false positive.
    Analysis: Python (pandas, SciPy, statsmodels). Methods: Welch t-test, delta method, bootstrap, CUPED, Holm correction, non-inferiority guardrails, sample-ratio-mismatch check.
