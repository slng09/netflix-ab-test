How many members does a test like this need?

The smallest lift a test can reliably detect (MDE) depends on sample size, significance level, power and variance reduction. Viewing hours vary a lot between members, so tests need large samples.

Recommendation and caveats

Recommend
  • Ship personalized artwork. Streaming hours and play starts both rose significantly across all members.
  • Forecast with the week-4 lift, not the 28-day average, because part of the early lift was novelty among tenured members.
  • Prioritise new members, whose lift is the largest and doesn't fade.
  • Ask engineering to recover the extra homepage load time, for example by pre-fetching artwork.
  • The playback-error blip (p ≈ 0.04) is a chance result inside its margin. Artwork doesn't touch the video player.
Limitations
  • All data is simulated with assumed effect sizes, so the numbers show the method, not a real Netflix outcome.
  • 28 days is too short to measure retention. A long-term holdout of about 5% of members would settle it.
  • Members share household profiles, so randomising by account may leak effects between viewers.
  • Filtered views are exploratory. With 60 possible filter combinations, some will look significant by chance.
Analysis: Python (pandas, SciPy, statsmodels). Methods: Welch t-test, delta method, CUPED, non-inferiority guardrails, sample-ratio-mismatch check. Time series use all assigned members (intent-to-treat), counting zero viewing after cancellation.
